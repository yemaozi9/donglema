适用于 iOS App「动了吗」· 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 6 日

一句话：动了吗不收集、不上传、不分享你的任何个人信息。你选择的视频和照片只在你的 iPhone 上处理，处理结果直接存回你的相册。

1. 我们处理哪些内容

  • 你主动选择的视频或实况照片：通过系统照片选择器选取，仅在本机用于生成锁屏 Live 图或分享版 Live 图。App 无法看到你没有选择的其他照片。
  • 保存到相册：生成后，App 仅申请「添加照片」权限，把结果写入你的相册，不读取相册内容。
  • 临时文件：处理过程中会在 App 的临时目录产生中间文件，App 启动或更换视频时会自动删除。

2. 我们不做的事

  • 不上传任何视频、照片或其他内容到服务器（App 没有服务器）。
  • 不要求注册或登录，不收集姓名、手机号、邮箱、位置、设备标识等信息。
  • 不使用任何第三方统计、广告或追踪 SDK，不进行跨 App 追踪。

3. 购买

「永久解锁」通过 Apple 的 App 内购买完成，付款与交易由 Apple 处理。App 只从 Apple 获取「是否已购买」的结果，用于解锁功能，无法获取你的付款信息。前 3 张免费次数只记录在你的设备本地。

4. 儿童

App 不面向特定年龄段收集信息，也不收集任何儿童个人信息。

5. 政策更新

如本政策有变更，我们会更新本页面并修改生效日期。

6. 联系我们

如有任何问题，请发送邮件至 donglema_app@163.com。

Privacy Policy

For the iOS app “Moved? Live Lock Screen” (动了吗) · Effective: November 6, 2026

In short: the app does not collect, upload or share any personal data. The videos and photos you choose are processed only on your iPhone, and the results are saved straight back to your photo library.

What we process

  • Videos or Live Photos you pick through the system photo picker, used on-device only to create Live wallpapers or shareable Live Photos. The app cannot see photos you did not pick.
  • Saving: the app only asks for “Add Photos” permission to save results. It does not read your library.
  • Temporary files created during processing are deleted automatically when the app starts or a new video is chosen.

What we don’t do

  • No uploads of any content — the app has no server.
  • No accounts or sign-in; no names, phone numbers, emails, location or device identifiers are collected.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs; no cross-app tracking.

Purchases

The “Unlock Forever” purchase is handled entirely by Apple. The app only receives whether the purchase is active and never sees your payment details. The 3 free saves are counted locally on your device.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
